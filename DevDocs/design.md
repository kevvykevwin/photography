# Camera workshop prototype

## Scope
A local, browser-only teaching prototype. Nikon F3 reference body, 28mm and 50mm teaching lenses, Nikkor 300mm f/4.5 AI-S ED-IF and Reflex-Nikkor 500mm f/8. Portra 400 and CineStill 800T. Four photographic scenario plates, a simulated exposure workflow, an inspectable 3D mechanism, and a scored retry loop.

## Design
Palette: paper #edf0ed, ink #202a28, muted #69736d, workbench #dce2dc, petrol #255e58, white #ffffff. System humanist sans body with Georgia display title; tabular setting values. Large camera and viewfinder occupy the first screen; quiet native controls underneath. The memorable element is the camera, not ornamental interface chrome.

Review: avoids a generic dashboard or landing-page hero; puts the actual learning controls within reach of their visual consequences. No scrolling animation or automatic model rotation. Mobile stacks the paired views.

## Accuracy boundaries
Camera geometry is an original schematic model, not a scanned F3 or service-manual reconstruction. 300mm internal focusing and 500mm mirror path are illustrative. Reflex generation is not yet confirmed. Photographic plates are real stock photographs, not samples made with these lenses or films. Same-position crop ratios are 28/f relative to an explicitly virtual 28mm plate, not asserted EXIF. Whole-plane blur cannot model scene depth or synthesize real background bokeh. Exposure EV, f-number, film sensitivity, horizontal angle of view and thin-lens depth of field are calculated; color rendering, shake, and motion appearance are approximations. No calibrated emulsion model, reciprocity, filters, perspective-changing camera motion or detailed shutter curtain simulation.
